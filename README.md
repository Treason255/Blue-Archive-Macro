# Blue Archive Macro
### A macro for automating dailies in Blue Archive
<br>

**requires <a href="https://www.autohotkey.com">AutoHotkey v2.0</a>**

# Info
I made this macro as a level 90 who is too lazy to do their dailies. This macro is not meant to "cheat" the game by getting any kind of advantage over other players, it is simply meant to automate typical daily tasks for rewards that pretty much all gacha games have.

To run use the macro, simply launch the .ahk file.

# Features

## Cafe
- Opens cafe and claims earnings

## Lesson
- Allows selection of areas and a set amount of lessons to complete

## Club
- Opens club for task

## Daily Pack
- Buys the free daily pack of 10k Credits and 10 AP

## Missions
- Selects multi-sweep preset tab(s) and sweeps the entered amount
- **You must select what mission you want to sweep in the multi-sweep tab.** Recommended to sweep a hard mission 3 times for the task.
- *input 99 for max sweep*

## Bounty
- Selects bounties and sweeps the entered amount
- *input 6 for max sweep*

## Commission
- Selects commissions and sweeps the entered amount
- *input 99 for max sweep*

## Scrimmage
- Selects the scrimmages and sweeps the entered amount
- *input 6 for max sweep*

## Tactical Challenge
- Battles an opponent 5 times
- Claims time and daily rewards

## Shop
- Purchases Advanced and Superior Activity Reports, Advanced and Superior Enhancement Stones, and Lesser and Normal Energy Drinks(total 90 AP)

## Recharge
- Purchases 120 AP the entered amount (currently limited to 3)

## Event
- Sweeps the 12th Mission of the currently ongoing event the entered amount
- *input 99 for max sweep*

## Tasks
- Claims rewards for completed tasks
- Claims the daily "Complete at least 8 Daily Tasks" task

## Mail
- Claims all unclaimed mail
